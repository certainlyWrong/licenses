# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04/06/2026

Welcome to **Sombras Digitais** (Digital Shadows)! Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data when you use it. By using **Sombras Digitais**, you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Data Collection

1.1. **Sombras Digitais** does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.  
1.2. The app does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to function.  
1.3. Player names entered during a game session are used solely within the app to identify participants and are stored only on the local device.

## 2. Locally Stored Data

2.1. The app stores match history on the device using local storage (SharedPreferences). This data includes player names used in past sessions, role assignments, round outcomes, and game results.  
2.2. This data never leaves the device. It is not uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third-party system.  
2.3. Users can permanently delete all stored match history from within the app via the History screen, or by clearing the app's data through the device's system settings.

## 3. Permissions

3.1. **Sombras Digitais** does not request access to sensitive device features such as the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or media library.  
3.2. The app does not require an internet connection to function and does not use it during normal gameplay.

## 4. Premium Content

4.1. Premium role unlocks are managed locally on the device. No payment information is processed by the app directly.  
4.2. Any transactions for premium content are handled exclusively by the platform through which the app was obtained (e.g., Google Play Store, Apple App Store), subject to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Third-Party Services

5.1. **Sombras Digitais** does not integrate analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other third-party SDK that collects user data.  
5.2. The app does not use tracking technologies of any kind.

## 6. Children's Privacy

6.1. The app is intended for players aged 13 and above.  
6.2. The app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is no special handling required for younger users beyond the age recommendation.

## 7. Data Security

7.1. Because **Sombras Digitais** does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of server-side data breaches or unauthorized remote access to user information.  
7.2. The security of locally stored data depends on the security of the user's device. Users are responsible for protecting access to their device.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

8.1. This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app's functionality or applicable legal requirements.  
8.2. If significant changes are made, a notification may be provided within the app or via the app's store listing.  
8.3. Continued use of the app after changes have been published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
